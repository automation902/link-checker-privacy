PRNEWS Link Checker - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

PRNEWS Link Checker ("the extension") is a browser extension operated by PRNEWS OÜ, Laeva tn 1, Kesklinna linnaosa, 10150 Tallinn, Estonia ("we"). It shows whether a website is available in the PRNEWS.IO catalog and at what price. This policy explains what the extension reads, what it sends, what it stores and how you can delete it.

What the extension reads

  • The current tab's domain, only when you click the extension icon. The extension reduces the address to its registrable domain (for example example.com).
  • Domains of links on Google search result pages and on Ahrefs report pages, only on those pages, only for results/rows visible on screen, and only to show the "In catalog" / "Not in catalog" badges.

The extension does not read your search queries, page text, form entries, cookies, passwords, browsing history or any page other than the ones listed above.

What is sent, and where

All requests go to one service, the PRNEWS.IO catalog API at us-central1-prnews-io.cloudfunctions.net.

WhenWhat is sent
You open the popup on a websiteThat website's domain
A Google results page or an Ahrefs report page is open (and the badge setting is on)The domains of the visible results or table rows, in batches
The extension needs the catalog linkAn anonymous configuration request (no personal data)
You press Submit on the "Add to catalog?" formThe domain and the email address you typed

Like any web request, these requests reveal your IP address and browser identifier to the server operator. Server logs are used only to operate and protect the service and are kept no longer than necessary for that purpose.

The email from the form is used only to contact you about the suggested website. Versions before 0.3.0 also sent the name you typed in that form; it is not used. We keep a suggestion until we have reviewed it and answered, and delete it on request.

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers, we use no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and the extension loads and runs no remote code.

What is stored on your device

Stored in the browser (chrome.storage) and never uploaded:

  • your settings (tooltip mode, badges on/off per site type, keep-history switch, and your appearance choice: system, light or dark; the popup also keeps a copy of that choice in its own local browser storage so it can show the right colors instantly);
  • the email you entered in the form, remembered after the first successful submission so you do not have to retype it (versions before 0.3.0 also remembered a name; the popup deletes it when it opens);
  • your favorites (domains you starred, with the last known status and price);
  • your history of checks you triggered yourself: opening the popup on a site, clicking a badge, starring a site (max 200 domains, newest first). Rows that are merely badged on Google/Ahrefs pages are not recorded;
  • a short-lived (10 minutes) cache of catalog answers.

Your choices and how to delete data

In the extension popup, open Settings (the gear icon):

  • turn off Keep history to stop recording history;
  • Clear saved email removes the remembered email address;
  • Clear all data removes history, favorites, the saved email and settings;
  • turn off badges for Google or Ahrefs separately.

History can also be cleared on the History screen (clock icon), and favorites removed one by one on the Favorites screen (star icon). Uninstalling the extension deletes all local data. To ask us to delete a suggestion you submitted (email, domain), write to the contact address below.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what data is collected, we will update the date above and note the change in the extension's release notes.
